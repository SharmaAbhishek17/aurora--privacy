01Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Aurora mobile application (Android and iOS), the website at auroranow.co and related services (together, the "Service"). The Service is operated by Sankar Group ("Aurora", "we", "us", "our"), Sankar Group, Malviya Nagar, New Delhi - 110017, India.

Aurora is a social discovery app for real-world plans. You post something you want to do in the next 48 hours, such as coffee, a walk, a movie, a meal, a workout, a short trip or a founder chat, and meet people nearby who want to do the same thing.

By creating an account, using the Service or granting device permissions, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service. This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: mobile number (used for OTP login), name, date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or over) and gender (optional).
  • Profile: photos, bio, interests, languages, profession or college (optional), and the city you use Aurora in.
  • Verification: if you choose to verify your profile, a live selfie or short video that we compare with your profile photos.
  • Plans and activity: plans you post or join, times, places you suggest, likes and requests.
  • Messages: chats with people you have connected with on a plan.
  • Safety reports: reports, blocks, feedback and anything you send to our support team.
  • Waitlist: the mobile number you enter on our website to join the waitlist.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: precise or approximate device location, only with your permission, to show plans near you.
  • Device data: device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, device identifiers and crash logs.
  • Usage data: screens viewed, features used, time spent and interactions, used to keep the app working and improve it.

Information from others

  • Payments: when you buy Aurora+, Google Play or the Apple App Store processes the payment and tells us whether it succeeded. We do not receive or store your full card, UPI or bank details.
  • Other users: reports or feedback other members submit about you.

04Location data

Location is central to Aurora, so we handle it carefully:

  • Other members see only an approximate area and distance for your plans, never your exact position.
  • The exact meeting place is shown only after both people confirm a plan.
  • Your home address is never shown.
  • You can switch location off in your device settings at any time. You can then set your city manually, but nearby results will be less accurate.

06How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these cases:

  • With other members: your profile, plans and messages are visible to members as the app intends. For example, your first name, photos and plan are visible to people nearby.
  • With service providers: cloud hosting, OTP and SMS delivery, analytics, crash reporting, customer support and verification tools. They process data only on our instructions and under confidentiality obligations.
  • With app stores: Google Play and Apple process subscription payments under their own privacy policies.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a government authority, or to protect someone's safety.
  • Business changes: if Aurora is merged, acquired or sells assets, your data may move to the new owner under this policy.

07Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as your account is active, or as long as needed for the purposes in this policy. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data as described in our Data Deletion Policy. Some records, such as payment records and safety reports, may be kept for longer where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and repeat abuse.

08Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data (most of it can be edited in the app);
  • delete your account and personal data (see Data Deletion);
  • withdraw consent and turn off permissions or marketing messages;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapacitated;
  • raise a grievance with us and, if it remains unresolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any right, email hello@auroranow.co from, or quoting, your registered mobile number.

09Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data. These include encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, limited staff access and regular reviews. No system is completely secure, so please keep your phone and OTPs private. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10Children's privacy

Aurora is only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a person under 18 has an account, we will delete it. If you believe a minor is using Aurora, please tell us at hello@auroranow.co.

11Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. Some service providers may process data outside India. When this happens, we make sure it is protected under this policy and that the transfer is permitted under Indian law.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are significant, we will also notify you in the app or by SMS before they take effect.
